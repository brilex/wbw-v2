If you've searched for website design pricing in Edmonton, Calgary, or Kelowna, you've probably seen numbers ranging from a few hundred dollars to tens of thousands. That range exists because "website" can mean a single template page or a fully custom-built platform — and the price should reflect which one you're actually getting.

Here's a realistic breakdown of what drives the cost and what to expect.

## What actually determines the price

Three things drive website design cost, regardless of location:

1. **Who builds it** — a DIY builder, a freelancer, or an agency with a real process
2. **What's included** — design only, or design plus copywriting, SEO, and performance work
3. **How custom it is** — a template with your logo swapped in, or a site built around your actual business

The most common mistake business owners make is comparing prices without comparing scope. A $300 site and a $1,500 site can look similar at a glance, but only one of them is built to actually get found on Google.

## Typical pricing by project type

### A basic business website (5 pages)

**Typical price: around $400 USD**

This covers a professional presence — home, about, services, portfolio, contact — with basic SEO foundations (meta tags, sitemap) and performance setup baked in, but not deep keyword research.

### A website with a real SEO strategy

**Typical price: around $700 USD**

This tier adds keyword research, a dedicated SEO-optimized page for your core service, and content optimization across every page. This is the difference between a site that exists and a site that actively brings in search traffic.

### A website with ongoing management

**Typical price: around $1,200 USD**

For businesses that want someone actively managing SEO after launch — monthly monitoring, reporting, and content updates — this tier typically includes ongoing hands-on management post-launch.
